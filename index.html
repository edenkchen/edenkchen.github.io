About Me

Born and raised in the Smoky Mountains of North Carolina, I grew up surrounded by nature — hiking trails, mountain air, and a deeply curious mind that loved strategy games and competition. Basketball courts and gaming sessions shaped how I think: quickly, collaboratively, and with an eye for what moves the needle.

Now at NYU Stern's Business, Technology & Entrepreneurship program, I've turned that competitive spirit into hands-on experience — serving as an Event Coordinator for NYU's Product Management Club, where I've helped run app-framework pitch sessions, competitive-analysis workshops, and user-research programming for students exploring product careers, myself included.

When I'm not organizing events, you'll find me co-leading orientation seminars for incoming Stern students, competing as a Starter on NYU's Valorant esports team, or out with a camera chasing a nature photo.
